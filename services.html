Services

The PGCHS Supply Office provides the following services:

1. Purchase Request

Processing of purchase requests for supplies, materials, equipment, and other requirements of the school.

2. Issuance of Common Supplies

Issuance of commonly used supplies and materials to authorized teaching and non-teaching personnel.

3. Issuance of Semi-expandable Equipment / Property

Processing and issuance of semi-expandable equipment and property to authorized personnel with the required documentation and accountability.

4. Signing of Clearance

Verification of applicable supply and property accountability for the signing of clearance documents.

5. Gate Pass

Processing of gate passes for the authorized movement or removal of school property and equipment from the school premises.

6. Return of Issued Items

Receiving and recording of supplies, equipment, and property returned to the Supply Office.

7. Request for Relief of Accountability

Processing of requests for relief of accountability for property in accordance with applicable rules and procedures.
