About the Supply Office

The Supply Office of Polangui General Comprehensive High School supports the school in the proper receipt, management, recording, safekeeping, and issuance of common supplies, equipment, and other accountable property needed for school operations.

The office helps ensure that supplies and equipment are properly received, documented, accounted for, and made available to authorized personnel and offices of the school.

Receiving of Supplies and Equipment

The Supply Office is responsible for receiving supplies, equipment, and other property delivered to the school. Upon receipt, the items are checked against the relevant procurement and delivery documents to ensure that the delivered items correspond with the approved request or purchase.

The receiving process includes checking the quantity, description, specifications, condition, and other pertinent details of the delivered items before they are accepted and recorded.

  • Receives delivered supplies and equipment.
  • Checks delivered items against supporting documents.
  • Verifies quantities and descriptions.
  • Checks the condition of delivered items.
  • Records received supplies and equipment for proper accountability and inventory management.

Canvassing and Procurement Support

The Supply Office assists in the procurement process by facilitating the canvassing and gathering of price and supplier information for needed supplies, equipment, and other requirements of the school, in accordance with applicable government procurement rules and procedures.

Canvassing helps provide the necessary information for the evaluation and processing of procurement requests and supports the school in obtaining supplies and equipment at appropriate prices and specifications.

  • Assists in gathering supplier and price information.
  • Facilitates canvassing for requested supplies and equipment.
  • Ensures that requested specifications are properly considered.
  • Maintains relevant procurement documentation.
  • Supports the processing and monitoring of procurement requests.

Issuance of Common Supplies and Equipment

The Supply Office facilitates the issuance of common supplies, semi-expandable equipment, and other school property to authorized personnel and offices based on approved requests and supporting documents.

Issuance is properly documented to ensure that supplies and property released by the school are recorded and that accountability is established where required.

  • Receives and evaluates requests for supplies and property.
  • Checks the availability of requested items.
  • Prepares and processes the appropriate issuance documents.
  • Releases approved supplies and equipment to authorized personnel.
  • Updates inventory and stock records after issuance.
  • Maintains records of issued supplies and property.

Inventory and Accountability

The Supply Office also helps maintain accurate inventory and accountability records of school supplies, equipment, and property. Proper documentation and recordkeeping help ensure that government property is protected, accounted for, and properly utilized.

Through systematic recording and monitoring, the office supports the school administration and personnel in maintaining reliable supply and property records.

Our Commitment

The PGCHS Supply Office is committed to providing organized, transparent, and accountable supply and property services to the school community. The office strives to ensure that necessary supplies and equipment are properly procured, received, recorded, stored, and issued to support the effective delivery of school operations and services.
